How to Play: Spot It! The Ugly Duckling Edition

Sample Prompt Preview: ugly duckling

When the game starts, a random word from your vocabulary list appears at the top of the screen and is read aloud automatically!

  1. 1
    Two Teams Side-by-Side: The screen is divided into two halves. Team 2 plays on the Left side, and Team 1 plays on the Right side.
  2. 2
    Move Your Spotlight: Each team starts with a round flashlight spotlight in the lower-left corner of their black screen. Drag your spotlight (with finger, stylus, or mouse) to explore the hidden anime farm & pond underneath.
  3. 3
    Find & Lock On to Score: Center your spotlight over the matching vocabulary illustration! Hold briefly as the green ring completes to earn +1 point.
  4. 4
    Automatic Reshuffle: Once found, a celebration chime plays, all pictures reshuffle to new non-overlapping positions, both spotlights return to their lower-left starting spots, and a new word is announced!
  5. 5
    Beat the Clock: The timer counts down to 0:00 (teachers can Pause/Resume anytime). When time runs out, the team with the highest score wins!
